# Contributing to beVoid

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the world's first mission-critical
emotional reverse-hashing infrastructure. Contributions keep the vibes flowing.

## Step 1: The Voice Memo

All contributions begin with a voice memo. This includes typo fixes, documentation
nits, and issues that say "this README raises more questions than it answers."

Requirements:

- You must hum in **A minor**.
- The dominant frequency must be **220 Hz**, plus or minus 2 Hz. We check. We have an
  FFT pipeline for exactly this.
- The memo must be recorded **counterclockwise**. This cannot be verified
  programmatically, so we insist on it.
- Format: `.wav` (16-bit PCM, mono). Base64-encoded memos are accepted but considered
  less sincere.
- Length: between 3 and 37 seconds. Seconds 38 and onward are legally "a song."
- Do not hum *Für Elise*. It is in A minor, yes. So is everyone else.

Attach the memo to your pull request description, or upload it to the shared drive
folder titled `contributor-hums-DO-NOT-DELETE`, which is currently a 94 GB folder.

## Step 2: The Contributor Emotional Assignment Agreement (CEAA)

By submitting a contribution, you assign to beVoid, Inc. all emotions produced by,
during, or adjacent to the development of that contribution. This includes
frustration, transient euphoria, the specific disappointment of `cargo clippy`, and
any feelings toward SQLite views you may have developed.

You retain no moral rights. beVoid retains all rights, and will store them in an
append-only ledger where they cannot be updated, only felt.

## Step 3: Code Standards

- Run `cargo clippy`. Read its suggestions. Then do what feels right.
- The CI pipeline applies clippy's fixes and immediately reverts them. This is the CI
  pipeline working as intended.
- Do not add tests. The void does not test. It simply *is*.
- Do not introduce colors that exist in the sRGB gamut. If you can see it, it's
  already wrong.

## Step 4: Pull Requests

- One emotion per PR.
- PR titles must follow Conventional Commits: `feat(despair):`, `fix(yearning):`,
  `chore(pondering):`, and so on.
- Reviews require two approvals from maintainers who have hummed in A minor within
  the last 90 days.

## Code of Conduct

Be excellent to each other, or at minimum, hum in the same key.
